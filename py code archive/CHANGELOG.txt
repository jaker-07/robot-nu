MAIN PROGRAMS (repository root)
- main_v19: v18 with logging removed. FRONT_TURN 8, MAX_CM 65, STOP 12.
- main_v20 (file header says v20g; v20a-c not kept): v19 driving plus task detection and tasks. Changes through v20d-g:
  - v20d: v19 driving kept, with a 60 s run timeout and everything switched off however the program ends. Buzzer made a plain on/off pin with the tone made in software (GP26 shares a PWM channel with ENA, so as PWM it buzzed whenever the motors ran).
  - v20e: After stopping, waits for the Task Decider's flag in front of the IR sensor (GP22). Buzzer 1 s, light, and spin (back up 0.5 s, pause 0.2 s, spin SPIN_TIME 1.5 s).
  - v20f: 5 ms gap between right and front pings (stray echoes faked a wall ahead). Pivot direction held until the front is clear.
  - v20g: Task chosen by how long the flag is held (about 3 s buzzer, 5 s light, 10 s spin), decided only after the flag goes. Holds under 2 s ignored.
- main_v21: CURRENT. Cleaned-up rewrite of v20g with the same driving. Task thresholds: under 4 s = buzzer, 4-7.5 s = light, 7.5 s and over = spin. Green start-up light sequence (about 2 s) plus 1 s before moving. Run timeout and maximum flag wait removed. Reported 14 Nov: completes the course and stops at the end in about 20 s. SPIN_TIME 1.5 s still needs tuning for exactly 360 deg.

KEY FINDINGS
- Holding the last good reading keeps navigation stable; weakening it (v9, v10) made runs worse.
- Logs showed v8's left sensor returning no echo, so v8 mainly follows the right wall plus front-sensor turns.
- Change one thing per test; bundled changes (v9) could not be traced to a cause.
- Until v13a the code read the left sensor on GP28/GP27, but the wiring had it on GP16/GP17. That is why the v8 and working10_11_2026 logs show the left sensor returning nothing.
- GP26 shares a PWM channel with ENA (GP10), so the buzzer cannot be a PWM pin while the motors run.
- Programs from before 11 Nov drive the right motor forward on IN4; after the motor flip it is IN3.
